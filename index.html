ONE PLAYER IS A SECRET MOLE

Everyone gets the same secret word, like PIZZA. Except one random player, the mole. The mole only sees the category ("Food") and has to bluff.

Nobody knows who the mole is. Your goal: if you know the word, find the mole. If you're the mole, don't get caught.

📌

STEP 1: GIVE ONE-WORD CLUES

Take turns typing one word that hints at the secret word. It's pinned to the board for everyone to see.

PIZZA → "cheese" · "Italy" · "slice"

Be clear enough that your team trusts you, but not so obvious that the mole figures out the word. The mole will try to sound like they know it. You go around twice.

🗣️

STEP 2: DISCUSS

Whose clue seemed vague or didn't fit? Say it out loud or in the chat. Press Ready when you've made up your mind.

"Sam said 'round'… that's a weird clue for pizza 🤔"
🗳️

STEP 3: VOTE

Tap the player you think is the mole. Optionally stake your reputation: +2 points if you're right, −1 if you're wrong.

If the mole is caught, they still get one last chance: guess the secret word from 6 options. Get it right and the mole wins anyway!

🏆

HOW YOU SCORE

🕵️ Mole escapes (not caught): mole +3
👥 Mole caught and guesses wrong: every other player +2
🎯 Mole caught but guesses the word: mole +2
🗳️ Naming the mole yourself: +1 (or +2 if staked)

Each round, a different player is the mole. Most points after all rounds wins.
